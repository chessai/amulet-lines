## Titan Lines

### Titan with 2 Amulets, No Other Resources

**Assumptions:** 6 bouncelands in deck, or 5 bouncelands in deck and 1 in graveyard.

1. Cast Titan. ETB search Mirrorpool and a bounceland.
2. Tap Mirrorpool once and the bounceland twice, then copy Titan with Mirrorpool.  
   **State:** 0 floating; Mirrorpool in graveyard.
3. Titan copy ETB. Search Hanweir Battlements and Gruul Turf, then use Hanweir twice to haste both Titans. Move to combat.  
   **State:** 0 floating; 0 untapped lands.
4. First Titan attack trigger: search The Mycosynth Gardens and Otawara. Float 1 mana with Mycosynth, then use it on the second untap to copy Amulet. Float 2 mana with Otawara.  
   **State:** 2 floating; 3 Amulets.
5. Second Titan attack trigger: search Echoing Deeps copying Mirrorpool and a bounceland. Float 2 mana from Echoing/Mirrorpool and 4 mana from the bounceland, leaving both Echoing/Mirrorpool and the bounceland untapped. Hold the bounceland bounce trigger.  
   **State:** 8 floating.
6. Copy Titan with Echoing/Mirrorpool for 5 mana.  
   **State:** 3 floating; Echoing Deeps in graveyard.
7. Titan copy ETB. Search Urza’s Cave and Shifting Woodland. Tap Cave twice and Woodland twice, leaving both untapped.  
   **State:** 7 floating; Cave untapped; Woodland untapped.
8. Resolve the held bounceland trigger, picking up Otawara. Channel Otawara targeting your original nontoken Titan.  
   **State:** 3 floating; Titan in hand.
9. Move to second main phase. Mana empties. You have:
   - 3 Amulets
   - Untapped bounceland
   - Untapped Urza’s Cave
   - Untapped Shifting Woodland
   - Titan in hand

   **State:** 0 floating.
10. Tap the bounceland for 2 and Shifting Woodland for 1. Activate Cave, finding a bounceland. Tap the new bounceland three times.  
    **State:** 6 floating.
11. Cast Titan from hand.  
    **State:** 0 floating.
12. Titan ETB. Search bounceland and Tolaria West. Tap bounceland three times and Tolaria West three times. Bounce Tolaria West.  
    **State:** 9 floating; TWest in hand.
13. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Aftermath Analyst. Cast and activate Analyst.  
    **State:** 0 floating; Analyst in graveyard.
14. Analyst returns at least Mirrorpool, Echoing Deeps, Cave, Otawara, and TWest. Echoing Deeps enters copying Urza’s Cave. Tap:
    - Mirrorpool three times
    - Otawara three times
    - TWest three times
    - Real Cave twice, leaving it untapped
    - Echoing/Cave twice, leaving it untapped

    **State:** 13 floating; 2 untapped Cave effects.
15. Activate a Cave, finding a bounceland. Tap the bounceland three times, then bounce TWest.  
    **State:** 16 floating; TWest in hand.
16. Transmute TWest for Zuran Orb and cast Zuran Orb.  
    **State:** 13 floating.
17. Activate the other Cave, finding Urza’s Saga. Tap it, then sacrifice it to Zuran Orb for delirium.  
    **State:** 13 floating.
18. Activate Shifting Woodland targeting Aftermath Analyst.  
    **State:** 9 floating.
19. Shifting Woodland becomes Analyst. Activate the copied Analyst.  
    **State:** 5 floating.
20. Analyst returns Shifting Woodland, Cave, Echoing/Cave, TWest, Mirrorpool, Otawara, Urza’s Saga, and any other sacrificed lands. Loop.

#### Cons

1. Does not work with Spelunking.
2. Dies to Consign/Solitude, as is typical for Titan + 2 Amulet lines.
3. Bounce-intensive. As written, asks for 6 bouncelands. New lines may be possible that cut this down.

## Two amulet titan kill that does not die to spelunking submitted by Epreez

### Starting Conditions
- Decklist changes:  Add Kavaron, Memorial World and a 2nd copy of echoing deeps/mirrorpool.
- Hand: None
- Graveyard: None
- Floating mana: None
- Land drop available: No
- Relevant library contents: 5 bouncelands, 2 Simic Growth Chamber, Kavaron, Mirrorpool, 1 Echoing deeps, 1 echoing deeps/mirrorpool, Tolaria West, Summoner's Pact, and Aftermath Analyst in deck.

### Line

1. Titan enters, grab Mirrorpool and a bounceland, copy titan. Repeat this process twice with bouncelands and Echoing deeps/Mirrorpool. Return the 3 bouncelands to your hand.
3. 3rd titan copy enters, grab Kavaron and Tolaria West, float 2 mana, leaving both lands untapped.
4. Station Kavaron twice, with 2 of the titans. Then activate Kavaron, sacrificing itself, giving both remaining titans haste.
5. Attack with both titans, first trigger grab Otawara and Gardens. Copy amulet with gardens using the mana from Otawara.
6. 2nd trigger, grab Urza's cave and SGC, float 4 mana from SGC, return Otawara. Bounce the nontoken titan to hand with Otawara.
7. 2nd main, crack cave using SGC and Tolaria West. Grab a bounceland, floating 6 mana and casting a titan. Pickup Tolaria West with the bounce trigger.
8. Grab Shifting Woodland and SGC, floating 9 mana, returning SGC with the bounce trigger. Transmute for pact, pact for analyst.
9. Cast and crack analyst, returning Mirrorpool, Tolaria West, Kavaron, Urza's Cave, and 2 Echoing deeps each copying Mirrorpool.
10. Float 15 mana, leaving Mirrorpool untapped. Copy titan with Mirrorpool, grabbing a bounceland and urza's saga, 17 mana, picking up Tolaria West with the bounce trigger. 
11. Transmute Tolaria West for zuran orb, sac urza's saga to zuran orb and start looping with 14 mana.

### Result

Analyst Loop post combat with the ability to grab any land from the deck and make infinite titans.

### Notes
Note: This line does not die to Spelunking because we station Kavaron and attack with 2 Titans. We can move to second main with 3 amulets, cave, and mana to activate because we enter combat with an extra land. Kavaron also sacrifices a bounceland so we only need 5 in deck.
Note 2: If we have a forest in play then Shifting Woodlands won't enter tapped in part 11, but if we have a forest in play at the start we can bounce it instead of a bounceland, so this isn't a problem.

### Titan with 2 Amulets and 4 Floating: Pre-Combat Win

1. Cast Titan. ETB search Mirrorpool and The Mycosynth Gardens. Tap Mirrorpool once, leaving it untapped. Tap Mycosynth once, then use that mana to activate Mycosynth copying Amulet. Copy Titan with Mirrorpool.  
   **State:** 0 floating; 3 Amulets; Mirrorpool in graveyard.
2. Titan copy ETB. Search a bounceland and Echoing Deeps copying Mirrorpool. Tap Echoing/Mirrorpool twice, leaving it untapped. Tap the bounceland three times. Hold the bounceland bounce trigger. Copy Titan with Echoing/Mirrorpool.  
   **State:** 3 floating; Echoing Deeps in graveyard; bounceland bounce still on stack.
3. Titan copy ETB. Search Urza’s Cave and Tolaria West. Tap Tolaria West three times. Tap Cave twice, leaving Cave untapped. Resolve the held bounceland trigger, bouncing Tolaria West.  
   **State:** 8 floating; Cave untapped; TWest in hand.
4. Activate Cave for a bounceland. Tap the bounceland three times.  
   **State:** 11 floating; Cave in graveyard.
5. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Aftermath Analyst. Cast and activate Analyst.  
   **State:** 2 floating; Analyst in graveyard.
6. Analyst returns Cave, Mirrorpool, Echoing Deeps, and Tolaria West. Echoing Deeps enters copying Mirrorpool. Tap Tolaria West three times. Tap Cave twice, leaving it untapped. Tap both Mirrorpools twice each, leaving both untapped.  
   **State:** 11 floating; Cave untapped; both Mirrorpools untapped.
7. Copy Titan with one Mirrorpool.  
   **State:** 6 floating; one Mirrorpool untapped.
8. Titan copy ETB. Search a bounceland and Shifting Woodland. Tap the bounceland three times and Woodland three times. Bounce Tolaria West.  
   **State:** 15 floating; TWest in hand; Woodland on battlefield.
9. Transmute TWest for Zuran Orb and cast Zuran Orb.  
   **State:** 12 floating.
10. Activate Cave for Urza’s Saga.  
    **State:** 12 floating; Saga on battlefield.
11. Sacrifice Urza’s Saga to Zuran Orb. Delirium is deterministic: land + enchantment from Saga, instant from Pact, creature from Analyst. Loop.

## Scapeshift Lines

### Scapeshift with 1 Amulet, 5 Lands, and Sac Outlet Access

**Requirement:** Zuran Orb in hand/on battlefield, or Sylvan Safekeeper already on battlefield.

1. Cast Scapeshift, sacrificing all 5 lands.  
   **State:** 1 floating.
2. Find TWest + Simic Growth Chamber + 3 Vestige/Bounce. Tap all, then bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 10 floating.
3. Sacrifice the remaining 4 lands.
4. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Aftermath Analyst. Cast and activate Analyst.  
   **State:** 1 floating.
5. Analyst returns the original 5 lands + TWest + Simic Growth Chamber + 3 Vestige/Bounce. Bounce TWest. If using Zuran Orb, sacrifice everything else.  
   **State:** 14 floating.
6. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Titan. Cast Titan.  
   **State:** 5 floating.
7. Titan ETB searches Bounce/Vestige + Shifting Woodland.  
   **State:** 8 floating.
8. Loop.

### Scapeshift with 1 Amulet and 6 Lands

#### Starting with 2 Extra Mana: Getting Mycosynth

1. Cast Scapeshift, sacrifice all 6 lands, and float 2 mana.  
   **State:** 2 floating.
2. Find Mycosynth Gardens + Urza’s Cave + TWest + Simic Growth Chamber + Bounce + Bounce.
3. Tap TWest and all 3 bounces.  
   **State:** 9 floating.
4. Resolve bounceland triggers returning TWest and 2 bounces.  
   **State:** 9 floating; TWest in hand; Gardens/Cave untapped.
5. Pay 1 and tap The Mycosynth Gardens to copy Amulet.  
   **State:** 8 floating; 2 Amulet effects.
6. Pay 3, tap and sacrifice Urza’s Cave, and find a Bounce. Tap and then bounce the Bounce.  
   **State:** 9 floating.
7. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Aftermath Analyst. Cast Analyst and activate it.  
   **State:** 0 floating.
8. Analyst returns the original 6 lands, TWest, and Urza’s Cave. Tap everything twice except Urza’s Cave only once.  
   **State:** 15 floating; Cave untapped.
9. Activate Urza’s Cave for a bounceland, and bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 16 floating; TWest in hand.
10. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Titan. Cast Titan.  
    **State:** 7 floating.
11. Titan ETB finds Shifting Woodland and a bounceland. Tap all for mana but keep the bounce trigger on the stack.  
    **State:** 13 floating.
12. Activate Shifting Woodland copying Aftermath Analyst, then activate Analyst.  
    **State:** 5 floating.
13. Analyst returns at least TWest, Cave, and Shifting Woodland.  
    **State:** 11+ floating.
14. Resolve the held bounceland trigger returning TWest.
15. Transmute TWest for Zuran Orb and cast Zuran Orb.  
    **State:** 8+ floating. Loop.

#### Starting with 2 Extra Mana: Using Cephalid Coliseum

1. Cast Scapeshift, sacrifice all 6 lands, and float 2 mana.  
   **State:** 2 floating.
2. Find TWest + Cephalid Coliseum + Shifting Woodland + Simic Growth Chamber + Simic Growth Chamber + Bounce. Tap all but Coliseum. Bounce TWest and 2 bouncelands.  
   **State:** 10 floating.
3. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact.  
   **State:** 7 floating.
4. Pact for Aftermath Analyst, then cast Aftermath Analyst.  
   **State:** 5 floating.
5. Activate Cephalid Coliseum, drawing 3 and discarding the 2 bounced bouncelands plus any card.  
   **State:** 4 floating; Coliseum + 2 bouncelands in graveyard.
6. Activate Aftermath Analyst.  
   **State:** 0 floating; delirium = Scapeshift, Pact, Analyst, lands.
7. Analyst returns the original 6 lands, TWest, Cephalid Coliseum, and the 2 discarded bouncelands. Tap everything, bounce TWest and any other land.  
   **State:** 12 floating.
8. From here, either transmute TWest for Zuran Orb, or for Pact -> Safekeeper. You have enough mana for either.
9. Cast whichever sac outlet you got. You have either 9 or 8 mana remaining. Loop.

### Scapeshift with 2 Amulets and 3 Lands

#### Bounce + Land + Land + 2 Extra Mana

This line requires only a single sol land and 2 extra starting mana, which is a more common position to be in.

1. Cast Scapeshift, sacrificing all 3 lands.  
   **State:** 2 floating.
2. Get TWest + Bounce + Cave. Tap Cave only once, then bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 9 floating.
3. Activate Cave, search Bounce, and bounce a Bounce.  
   **State:** 10 floating.
4. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Analyst. Cast and activate Analyst.  
   **State:** 1 floating.
5. Analyst returns Bounce + TWest + Cave + Land + Land. Tap Cave only once and bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 12 floating.
6. Transmute TWest for Orb and cast Orb.  
   **State:** 9 floating.
7. Activate Cave, search Woodland.  
   **State:** 8 floating.
8. Loop.

#### Bounce + Vestige/Bounce + Land + 1 Extra Mana

1. Cast Scapeshift, sacrificing all 3 lands.  
   **State:** 1 floating.
2. Get TWest + Bounce + Cave. Tap Cave only once and everything else twice, then bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 8 floating.
3. Activate Cave, search Bounce, and bounce a Bounce.  
   **State:** 9 floating.
4. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Analyst. Cast and activate Analyst.  
   **State:** 0 floating.
5. Analyst returns Bounce + Vestige/Bounce + Land + Cave + TWest. Tap Cave only once and everything else twice. Resolve bounces, bouncing at least TWest.  
   **State:** 12–13 floating.
6. Transmute TWest for Zuran Orb and cast Zuran Orb.  
   **State:** 9–10 floating.
7. Activate Cave, search Shifting Woodland.  
   **State:** 8–9 floating.
8. Loop.

### Scapeshift with 2 Amulets and 4 Lands

#### Any 4 Lands Other Than Cave/TWest

1. Cast Scapeshift, sacrificing all 4 lands.  
   **State:** 0 floating.
2. Get TWest + Mycosynth + Urza’s Cave + Bounce. Tap everything twice except Cave and Mycosynth, then bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 8 floating.
3. Pay 1 and tap Mycosynth to make it a copy of an Amulet.  
   **State:** 7 floating.
4. Activate Cave, getting another bounceland. Bounce whatever; if they Consign the original bounce trigger, you can attempt to bounce TWest again here.  
   **State:** 10 floating.
5. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Analyst. Cast Analyst and activate it.  
   **State:** 1 floating.
6. Analyst returns 4 original lands, Cave, and TWest. Tap Cave only twice.  
   **State:** 18 floating.
7. Activate Cave, finding a Bounce, and bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 21 floating.
8. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Titan. Cast Titan.  
   **State:** 12 floating.
9. Titan finds Shifting Woodland + X.  
   **State:** 18+ floating.
10. Activate Woodland to copy Analyst, then activate Analyst.  
    **State:** 10+ floating.
11. Analyst returns Woodland + Cave + TWest. Tap Cave only twice.  
    **State:** 18+ floating.
12. Activate Cave, finding Bounce, and bounce TWest.  
    **State:** 24 floating.
13. Transmute TWest for Orb.  
    **State:** 21 floating. Loop.

#### Vestige or Bounceland + Any 3 Lands Other Than TWest/Cave

**Starting position:** Vestige/Bounce + 3 Forests.

**Result:** Combos with exact mana and no Mycosynth.

1. Cast Scapeshift, sacrificing all 4 lands.  
   **State:** 0 floating.
2. Get TWest + Urza’s Cave + Bounce + Bounce. Tap everything twice but Cave only once, then bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 11 floating.
3. Activate Cave, finding Bounce.  
   **State:** 12 floating.
4. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Analyst. Cast and activate Analyst.  
   **State:** 3 floating.
5. Analyst returns Vestige + 3 Forests + TWest + Cave. Tap all twice but Cave only once. Make green from Vestige.  
   **State:** 15 floating.
6. Activate Cave, finding Bounce, and bounce TWest.  
   **State:** 16 floating.
7. Transmute TWest for Summoner’s Pact. Pact for Titan. Cast Titan.  
   **State:** 7 floating.
8. Titan ETB finds Woodland + Bounce, bouncing something nonessential.  
   **State:** 13 floating.
9. Activate Woodland copying Analyst, then activate Analyst.  
   **State:** 5 floating.
10. Analyst returns TWest, Cave, and Woodland. Tap all twice but Cave only once.  
    **State:** 10 floating.
11. Activate Cave for Bounce, and bounce TWest.  
    **State:** 11 floating.
12. Transmute TWest for Orb.  
    **State:** 8 floating.
13. Cast Orb. You now have the exact loop.

## Tips and Tricks

This section collects reusable tactical patterns that show up across Amulet combo lines. These are not full lines by themselves. They are small conversion rules, contingency plans, mana thresholds, and stack patterns that can be reused inside many different lines.

### Zuran Orb in Graveyard

If `Zuran Orb` is in your graveyard, you can still loop with it if you have `Shifting Woodland`, `Aftermath Analyst` in the graveyard, delirium, and enough mana to activate Woodland twice and then activate Analyst (so, >= 12 mana).

The generic pattern is:

1. Activate `Shifting Woodland` targeting `Aftermath Analyst`.
2. Hold priority, activate `Shifting Woodland` targeting `Zuran Orb`.
3. Let the Woodland activation targeting `Zuran Orb` resolve.
4. Woodland is now a copy of `Zuran Orb`; sacrifice your other lands.
5. Let the Woodland activation targeting `Aftermath Analyst` resolve.
6. Activate Woodland-as-Analyst to return Woodland + the sacrificed lands.
7. Repeat if the returned lands produce enough mana to pay for the next cycle.

### Sacrifice Outlet Exiled

If your sac outlet (e.g. `Zuran Orb`) is in exile, you can replace your sac outlet by using Scapeshift and infinitely copying Scapeshift with Mirrorpool.

The pattern is:

1. Cast Scapeshift.
2. Hold priority, activate Mirrorpool targeting Scapeshift.
3. Allow the Scapeshift copy to sacrifice whatever lands, then searching whatever lands.
4. Repeat.

### Safekeeper as a Sac Outlet

`Sylvan Safekeeper` can function as a repeatable land-sacrifice outlet, but it has an important targeting constraint: once Safekeeper's ability has resolved and gives a creature shroud, that creature cannot be targeted again. In practice this is easy because while comboing you will often just produce new creatures, but you don't want to make a simple mistake.

Useful patterns:

- Remember that you can sacrifice multiple lands against the same target by holding priority.
- You can target Safekeeper itself to sacrifice lands, but then Safekeeper has shroud.
- If Safekeeper has shroud, you need another creature to target in order to sacrifice more lands.
- Common extra targets include `Arboreal Grazer`, a Saga Construct, `Primeval Titan`, or a creature-land line involving `Dryad Arbor` if that is part of the position.
- When `Shifting Woodland` is copying `Aftermath Analyst`, Safekeeper can target Woodland-as-Analyst. You can sacrifice lands with the Safekeeper ability on the stack, then activate the Analyst ability to return them.

### Urza's Cave Conversion Patterns

`Urza's Cave` converts spare mana into whatever land the line is missing.

Common Cave finds include:

- A bounceland, usually to generate more mana (if multiple amulets) and/or bounce `TWest`.
- `Shifting Woodland`, usually to start the Analyst loop.
- `Urza's Saga`, often to provide an enchantment for delirium as a land that can be sacrified.
- A utility land such as `Otawara` or `Boseiju`.

### Bounceland Trigger Management

Bounceland triggers are often used as delayed resources rather than drawbacks.

Common uses:

- Bounce `TWest` after tapping it so it can be transmuted.
- Hold a bounce trigger on the stack while using mana or resolving other tutors.
- Bounce a land that needs to be replayed or discarded later, if the line allows that.
- Use multiple Amulet triggers to tap a bounceland several times before resolving its bounce trigger.

When checking a line, track whether the bounceland trigger has resolved. Many lines depend on a held trigger to return `TWest` only after enough mana has been produced.

### Cephalid Coliseum as a Controlled Discard Outlet

`Cephalid Coliseum` can convert excess mana and threshold into a controlled discard step.

The common use is:

1. Generate enough mana to activate Coliseum.
2. Use Coliseum to draw three and discard three.
3. Ignore the drawn cards if the line does not rely on them.
4. Discard lands that need to be in the graveyard for `Aftermath Analyst`.
